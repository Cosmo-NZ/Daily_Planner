Daily Pages

Each day has a Daily Plan page (tasks, schedule and reminders) linked to lined Daily Notes pages.

Preparing…
Every page is hyperlinked: month tabs open a calendar page for that month where every date links to its day, the week strip jumps between days, the arrows step a week (or a month on calendar pages), and each Daily Plan page links to its Daily Notes pages and back. Sized 540×720 pt (3:4) — ideal for Boox and similar e-ink tablets.
